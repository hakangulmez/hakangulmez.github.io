# Hakan Zeki Gülmez — research portfolio

This repository contains the source for [my portfolio](https://hakangulmez.github.io/), with research summaries and an essay on AI policy.

- [Master's thesis](https://hakangulmez.github.io/thesis/): the research question, measurement framework, empirical design, findings and limitations of *Generative AI as a Task Shock*.
- [PCT dataset](https://hakangulmez.github.io/pct/): how I constructed linked product, capability and customer-task records from annual reports, with examples and repeatability checks.
- [AI policy essay](https://hakangulmez.github.io/ai-policy/): analysis of AI policy and innovation systems.

The site uses static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Each page is in its own `index.html`; thesis figures are under `thesis/img/`. The pages present research outputs. The PCT dataset is available on request for research use.
