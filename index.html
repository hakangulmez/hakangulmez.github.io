Master’s thesis

Generative AI as a Task Shock: Product-Level LLM Substitution in B2B Software Markets

TU Munich · Master's thesis · 2026

Can frontier AI perform the tasks that business customers buy software to do, and how are software suppliers adjusting? I build a product–capability–task dataset from the business descriptions in 1,122 SEC annual reports, covering 14,435 customer-facing tasks. Exposure is scored against dated AI capability evidence, with each firm’s 2022 task basket held fixed.

Measured exposure more than triples by 2025. A continuous-treatment difference-in-differences design finds no statistically distinguishable same-period differential revenue response. The negative association with one-year-lagged exposure weakens when firm-specific trends are included. More exposed suppliers also disclose more substantive AI functionality, conditional on initial functionality. These results describe early adjustment; they do not rule out later substitution.

MacroLab: Cross-Country Macro Data and Econometrics in the Browser

Open-source tool · v2.4 · 38 OECD economies & euro area · 2026
36 indicators (33 national + 3 global) from 7 official sources; 25 validated methods spanning VAR, local projections, panel FE/LP, BVAR, forecasting, cointegration/VECM and break tests. 9 templates include the pooled ECB shock and EA inflation horse race. The dataset explorer, relationship scatters and forecast-evaluation charts run in the browser; results report the actual sample and sensitivity. A non-commercial research and teaching tool; model scenarios are not forecasts.

Local Economic Conditions and Public–Lender Loss Sharing in SBA 7(a) Lending

Applied research project · 2026
Official SBA loan-level data on recorded charge-off within 36 months. Firth logistic scorecards and machine-learning benchmarks show modest out-of-time discrimination and substantial calibration failures across cohorts. A separate macro-conditioned exercise estimates associations and assumption-based public–lender loss sharing under fixed scenarios; it is not a causal evaluation or a real-time forecast.

Monetary Policy Responses: Identification and Estimator Choice

Euro area & Türkiye · 2026
How much do monetary-policy response estimates depend on identification and estimator choice? Recursive, sign-restricted, and proxy VARs are compared with local projections using recursive shocks. A common euro-area sample holds settings fixed within two lag panels. Proxy estimates show euro appreciation, while price responses remain uncertain; instrument validity and conditional inference limit interpretation.

Inflation Forecasting and GDP Nowcasting Under Publication Delays

Euro area, Türkiye & Germany · 2026
Fixed inflation models are evaluated against simple benchmarks with expanding windows and explicit assumed publication lags. The comparison does not establish a general gain over a random walk. German GDP bridge, MIDAS, and factor nowcasts show rankings that depend on pandemic quarters. Release masks on latest-vintage data approximate information timing rather than reconstruct historical vintages.

Regularization Bias, Double Machine Learning, and Causal Assumptions

Monte Carlo & observational applications · 2026
Seeded simulations compare OLS, plug-in machine learning, and cross-fitted double machine learning. Orthogonalization reduces bias sharply in the sparse design, but learner rankings are not universal. Historical 401(k) and staggered-adoption applications examine propensity overlap, unobserved-confounding sensitivity, and cohort balance alongside the assumptions needed for causal interpretation.

Lockdowns and Urban Air Quality

Italy & Japan · Research paper · 2025
A matched city-level study of PM₂.₅ outcomes during COVID-19 restrictions. Propensity-score matching and difference-in-differences combine air-quality observations, policy stringency, and weather controls. Country-level, city-day, and fixed city-pair comparisons include balance, pre-trend, and placebo diagnostics. Interpretation depends on parallel trends and the comparability of the two countries.
